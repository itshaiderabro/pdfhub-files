PART-I: ENGLISH (20 Marks)  Vocabulary  Q1. Correct answer: (D) Extremely careful and precise  Meticulous means paying great attention to detail. Close synonyms are scrupulous, painstaking and thorough. The other options describe the opposite (careless/hasty) or have nothing to do with detail. Trap: FPSC often places the antonym among the options, so confirm whether the question asks for a synonym or an antonym.  Q2. Correct answer: (D) Plentiful  Scarce means in short supply. Rare, limited and insufficient are all synonyms of scarce, so they cannot be antonyms. Plentiful (abundant) is the true opposite. Tip: when three options mean the same thing, the odd one out is usually the answer for an antonym question.  Q3. Correct answer: (A) Frank and honest  Candid means truthful and straightforward in speech. It is not the same as rude, so 'impolite' is a trap. Secretive is nearly its opposite. Remember: candid = frank = open = sincere.  Q4. Correct answer: (C) Lasting for a very short time  Ephemeral comes from a Greek word meaning 'lasting only a day'. Things like morning dew or a mayfly's life are ephemeral. Its opposite is permanent or eternal. Trap: do not confuse it with 'ethereal' (light, delicate, heavenly).  Q5. Correct answer: (D) Malevolent  Benevolent (bene = good) means kind and well-meaning. Malevolent (mal = bad) means wishing harm to others. Kind, charitable and generous are synonyms of benevolent. Memory aid: the Latin roots bene = good, mal = bad.  Q6. Correct answer: (D) To avoid coming to the main point directly  The idiom means talking about unimportant things instead of getting to the point. It has no literal link with plants or fighting.
